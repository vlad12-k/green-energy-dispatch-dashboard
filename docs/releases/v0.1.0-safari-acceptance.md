## Exercised workflow and observed output

1. Opened the live workbench and used **Load synthetic example** for `synthetic-carbon-shift-demo`.
2. Changed `van-1` required energy to **8.5 kWh** and the second interval's intensity to **50 gCO2/kWh**; the first interval remained **400 gCO2/kWh**.
3. The desktop WebDriver workflow completed a simulation; this run is not treated as owner-verified pointer activation. In the mobile WebDriver session, pointer clicks on the top-bar **Run simulation** and empty-state **Evaluate this scenario** controls returned without starting a request; keyboard **Enter** on **Evaluate this scenario** successfully invoked `/api/v1/simulations/ev-depot/evaluate` and rendered **EVALUATION COMPLETE**.
4. Both strategies delivered **8.5 kWh**, with **0 kWh unmet** and **7 kW** peak depot power. The equal-service comparison was labelled **COMPARABLE**. Synthetic energy proxies were **2,875 gCO2** for the immediate baseline and **950 gCO2** for the carbon-aware heuristic, with a displayed baseline-minus-heuristic delta of **+1,925 gCO2** (**+66.956522%**). These are synthetic intensity-weighted proxies, not measured emissions, forecasts, or actual savings.
5. Inspected both schedule charts and their allocation/load tables, the carbon-intensity chart and interval table, comparison, provenance, and simulation-only notices. On mobile, keyboard disclosures exposed the interval, allocation, load, constraint, and per-vehicle details; table regions retained labelled names and keyboard focusability. The UI identified the profile as `synthetic:carbon-shift-demo-v1`, stated that no NESO data was used, and displayed **NO LIVE CONTROL**.
6. Keyboard activation of **Schedules** moved to `#schedule-details-heading`; **Space** toggled a table disclosure, and **Tab** moved to its labelled, focusable table region.
7. At both viewports, the document width matched the CSS viewport. On mobile, the two schedule cards stacked in one column. Schedule charts and some wide tables use their own labelled horizontal-scroll regions; vertical scrolling is expected.
8. No rendered `[role=alert]` messages or page `error`/`unhandledrejection` events were observed during the exercised path. The Safari WebDriver page reported `document.hasFocus() === false`; therefore its pointer-click results are inconclusive and do not establish focus-ring visibility or pointer activation. They are not a confirmed product defect. The later owner-verified desktop mouse interaction is recorded above; visible focus indication and mobile mouse interaction remain unverified.

The initial read-only `GET /health` timed out. One bounded retry was attempted after a possible cold start, but its exact response was not retained in the session evidence, so the retry outcome is **unverified**. The public root returned HTTP 200; that does not establish the Render Dashboard's current service status or configuration.
